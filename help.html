Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?
Track your order in real-time from your order history or from the success confirmation page. We provide live updates on preparation and delivery partner's location.
Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled within 60 seconds of placement. After this, the restaurant begins preparation and cancellation may not be possible. Contact support for assistance.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. For eligible cases, credits are added within 24 hours. Raise a request through the order details page.
What are Zomato Gold benefits?
Gold members enjoy unlimited free delivery on all orders, exclusive discounts at 15,000+ partner restaurants, priority support, and early access to new features and offers.
How does the referral program work?
Share your unique referral code with friends. When they sign up and place their first order, both of you receive ₹500 in Zomato Credits automatically.
Why is my payment failing?
Payment failures can happen due to insufficient balance, incorrect card details, or bank security checks. Try a different payment method or contact your bank. Zomato does not charge you for failed transactions.
